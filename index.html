Repeated Collisions · Question 03

Two balls between two walls.

(1) What are the velocities immediately after the first A–B collision?

(2) How much time passes before B first collides with the right wall?

(3) After reflecting from the right wall, will B collide with A again? If so, where?

(4) From the start until the second A–B collision, what total distance does A travel?
